Working with Me

I am interested in working with motivated undergraduate, master’s, and PhD students who are excited about ML systems and networking research. I typically work at the boundary between networked systems and machine learning, combining algorithm–system co-design with experimental evaluation and measurement-driven analysis. The best way to understand the scope and style of the work is to browse recent papers.

  • Undergraduate and Master’s students: Prior coursework in networking and AI/ML systems is helpful, as is hands-on experience (e.g., projects, internships, or prior research). A strong systems mindset matters more than familiarity with any specific toolchain.

Zero-Touch Networking

Bringing AI into control and management loops to enable closed-loop automation: telemetry-driven monitoring, anomaly/threat detection, and intent-based resource orchestration for NextG networks.

Resource-Aware Machine Learning

Designing learning methods that remain reliable under strict latency, bandwidth, and energy budgets. This includes lightweight adaptation, robustness to noisy telemetry, and deployment-aware training for RAN, edge, and optical platforms.

Federated Learning & In-Network Aggregation

Treating the communication infrastructure as part of the learning system by embedding aggregation and coordination inside the network. This includes in-network aggregation for scalable FL, network-assisted scheduling and client selection, and principled trade-offs between consistency and time-to-accuracy at scale.
