Some of the research topics I am currently exploring include:

  • Resource-aware machine learning under latency, bandwidth, and energy constraints in RAN, edge, and optical systems.
  • Federated learning systems with in-network aggregation, network-assisted scheduling, and scalable coordination.
  • Network–learning co-design, treating the communication infrastructure as an active component of distributed intelligence.

Working with Me

I am interested in working with motivated undergraduate, master’s, and PhD students who are excited about ML systems and networking research. I typically work at the boundary between networked systems and machine learning, combining algorithm–system co-design with experimental evaluation and measurement-driven analysis. The best way to understand the scope and style of the work is to browse recent papers.
