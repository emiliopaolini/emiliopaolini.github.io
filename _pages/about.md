I am an Assistant Professor at Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, networking, and distributed systems. His research focuses on AI-native communication infrastructures, spanning radio access networks and optical networks, with particular emphasis on federated learning, in-network aggregation, and resource-aware machine learning.

Much of my work explores how learning algorithms can be co-designed with network architectures, treating the network not merely as a transport but as an active participant in distributed intelligence.
